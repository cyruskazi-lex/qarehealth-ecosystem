Health impact, measured and tokenised.

This is how the ecosystem scales. The same records that run care also produce a signed, auditable measure of health impact. Verified impact becomes an asset institutional capital can fund, the way Helios does it for energy and Stratum for mining.

1
Lexington sets the framework
An EESG Health Impact assessment defines the benchmarks a health entity must meet before any system is deployed.
2
Qäre measures against it
The Health Impact Analysis reads live records, not surveys, and scores each entity continuously on the framework.
3
Independent verifiers sign
Evidence routes to independent verifiers. The result is a signed Verified Health Outcome Statement, hash-chained and tamper-evident.
4
Impact is tokenised on the Commons
Signed outcomes publish to the Global Impact Commons as impact tokens, anonymised and competitor-blind.
5
Capital connects
DFIs, impact funds and donors fund verified outcomes directly. Data acquisition cost falls toward zero as the network grows.

Why it compounds

Network effect

Every entity that joins makes the impact picture more complete and the verification cheaper per record.

Real-time, not sampled

Impact is computed from the live exchange, replacing lagging quarterly survey data.

Owned, then shared

Entities own their data. Only anonymised, verified aggregates ever reach the Commons.

One Commons, four instruments

Health, energy, mining and land publish to the same Commons, so capital reads one standard.

Ownership and privacy are structural, not promised.

Every surface states what it touches. Nothing is sold out the back. The two things that cannot bend are neutrality and privacy, because if either breaks, adoption stops and no amount of sponsor money brings it back.

Double-key consent

Care-coordination consent and secondary-use consent are separate grants that can never be collapsed into one.

Append-only ledger

The consent ledger and audit log are hash-chained and tamper-evident. Last-writer-wins is forbidden for clinical data.

Separation of duties

A consent steward can never also hold a data-consuming role. The structure enforces it, not a policy memo.

IRIS advises, a human decides

The agent explains and predicts from the record. Every clinical decision is made by a licensed person.

IRIS reads the record, and names the specialist behind the answer.

One intelligence across every product. It describes what is happening, predicts what comes next, and prescribes what to do, always grounded in the records and always pointing to the source. Florence routes the specialists underneath; the user sees IRIS.
