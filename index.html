Twelve health apps. One record underneath.

Qäre Health connects doctors, pharmacies, labs, hospitals, ministries and manufacturers on one exchange. Write a prescription in one app and it appears in the others at once. Every institution measures its health impact, and with consent publishes it verified and traceable to the Global Impact Commons, where it can raise capital. Nothing is unverified or untraceable.

See the interconnection, live

Open these four in separate browser tabs. In Pulse, prescribe CardiNex-10 for Amara. Watch it appear in Cura as a dispensing task, then dispense it and watch demand land in Nexus and the record update in Nova. The exchange is shared across your tabs.
