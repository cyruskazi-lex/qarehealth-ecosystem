# Health impact, measured and tokenised

The scaling thesis of the ecosystem. The same records that run care produce a
signed, auditable measure of health impact. Verified impact becomes an asset
institutional capital can fund, exactly as Helios does it for energy and Stratum
for mining, publishing to the same Global Impact Commons so capital reads one
standard across all four instruments.

## The five steps

1. **Lexington sets the framework.** An EESG Health Impact assessment defines the
   benchmarks a health entity must meet. This is the first phase of every
   engagement and it designs the system before anything is deployed.

2. **Qäre measures against it.** The Health Impact Analysis reads the live
   exchange, not survey samples, and scores each entity continuously against the
   framework. Because it reads real records, it replaces lagging quarterly data.

3. **Independent verifiers sign.** Evidence routes to independent verifiers. The
   output is a Verified Health Outcome Statement: hash-chained, tamper-evident,
   and stripped of patient and individual-provider identity.

4. **Impact is tokenised on the Commons.** Signed statements publish to the
   Global Impact Commons as impact tokens. They are anonymised and
   competitor-blind, so an entity can prove its outcomes without exposing its
   data to rivals.

5. **Capital connects.** Development finance institutions, impact funds and
   donors fund verified outcomes directly against the token. Data-acquisition
   cost falls toward zero as the network grows, and the value of the picture
   grows with every entity that joins.

## Why it compounds

- **Network effect.** Every entity that joins makes the impact picture more
  complete and lowers the verification cost per record.
- **Real-time, not sampled.** Impact is computed from the live exchange.
- **Owned, then shared.** Entities own their data; only anonymised, verified
  aggregates ever reach the Commons.
- **One Commons, four instruments.** Health, energy, mining and land publish to
  the same Commons, so one capital standard spans the ecosystem.

## What the demo must show

- The Health Impact Analysis surface, reading seeded live records.
- A Verified Health Outcome Statement with its signature and provenance.
- The token on the Commons and the capital that subscribes to it.
- The consent and anonymisation boundary that makes it safe to publish.

## EESG, not ESG

The framework is EESG: the leading extra E is the entity's own economic and
operational health, measured alongside Environmental, Social and Governance, so
impact and viability are scored together rather than treated as a charity line.
Confirm the exact EESG health metric set with Lexington before external use.
